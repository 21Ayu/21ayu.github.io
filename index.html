    About Me

    Analytical & Solutions-Oriented Business Analyst

    My Business Analysis Journey

    My path to business analysis is unconventional — and that's my edge. With a background in Civil Engineering and experience at the UPSC interview stage, I built deeply structured thinking and multi-stakeholder communication skills long before I entered the corporate world. Engineering taught me to map systems methodically; UPSC taught me to synthesize vast information and communicate it to diverse audiences — both are core to what great business analysts do every day.

    My transition into BA happened when I realised my passion lay in asking the right questions, documenting the right requirements, and ensuring technology solves the actual business problem. I self-taught data science, won a hackathon, and dove into the world of requirements engineering, process mapping, and analytics. That curiosity-driven rigour continues to define how I work today.

    💡 My BA Philosophy

    "Start with the 'why', map the process, gather the requirements, validate with data, and measure impact. Great business analysis bridges the gap between what stakeholders say they want and what the business actually needs. My role is to ask the right questions, write unambiguous requirements, and ensure every solution delivers clear, measurable value."

    Currently at PLEM (founded by BharatPe CXOs), I drive end-to-end business analysis for a next-gen brand rewards and loyalty ecosystem. I bridge business stakeholders and engineering teams through detailed BRDs, process maps, and data-backed recommendations. Whether redesigning workflows that cut processing time 10x or building analytics dashboards that improved merchant conversion by 15%, I focus on turning ambiguous business problems into actionable, well-documented solutions.
